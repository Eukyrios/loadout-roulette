Loadout Roulette

A random loadout generator for Delta Force: Operations. Five stages, in order:

  1. Spin the roulette wheel for a difficulty — Easy, Normal or Hard — and take the token.
  2. Feed the token into the slot machine and pull for your kit: map, operator, primary weapon, helmet, vest, chest rig and backpack, filtered to what that difficulty actually allows.
  3. Throw the dice for your loadout and attachment spending caps.
  4. Draw up to five keycards, face down, from the locked rooms on the map you rolled.
  5. Draw a stick for squad size: solo, duo or trio.
